Our story

Built by engineers who got tired of IT that only shows up when something breaks.

Nexova IT started in 2011 as a two-person help desk for a handful of local businesses in Columbus. Today we're a full managed-services team — but the job hasn't changed: keep your systems running, keep your data safe, and pick up the phone when it matters.

  1. 2011

    Founded in Columbus

    Started as a two-person help desk serving five local businesses out of a single office.

  2. 2014

    First enterprise cloud migration

    Moved a 200-seat logistics client to the cloud with zero unplanned downtime, and built our migration playbook around it.

  3. 2018

    Dedicated security practice launched

    Stood up a standalone cybersecurity team offering continuous monitoring, audits, and incident response.

  4. 2021

    100th client milestone

    Crossed 100 supported organizations while holding response-time guarantees steady.

  5. 2025

    500+ organizations, one standard

    Now supporting 500+ clients across the region with the same response-time guarantees we started with.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast do you respond to support tickets?

Critical issues are acknowledged within 15 minutes, 24/7. Standard tickets are acknowledged within 2 business hours. Response-time targets are written into every service agreement.

Do you work with our existing IT staff, or replace them?

Both models work. Most clients keep an internal point of contact and use us for 24/7 monitoring, specialized security work, and overflow capacity. Fully outsourced IT is also available.

What size companies do you work with?

Typically 20 to 500 employees. We're set up for organizations that need enterprise-grade reliability and security without an in-house team large enough to cover it around the clock.

How long does a cloud migration take?

Most migrations run 6 to 12 weeks depending on scope, with a phased cutover plan and a documented rollback path at every stage — timelines are confirmed after the initial assessment.

Is the initial consultation actually free?

Yes. It's a 30-minute call to understand your environment and constraints, followed by a written recommendation. No obligation to sign anything.
