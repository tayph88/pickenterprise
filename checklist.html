IT Security Readiness Checklist

The same 10-point review our engineers run during a new-client security assessment. Work through it in under 10 minutes.

Access & Identity

Multi-factor authentication is enforced on email, VPN, and admin accounts.

Former employees' accounts are disabled within 24 hours of departure.

Admin/privileged access is limited to the people who actually need it.

Backups & Recovery

Backups run automatically and at least one copy is offline or immutable.

A restore has actually been tested in the last 90 days — not just verified as "completed."

Patching & Configuration

Critical security patches are applied within 14 days of release.

No systems are running on end-of-life operating systems or software.

Monitoring & Detection

Login attempts and admin actions are logged somewhere you'd actually notice an anomaly.

Someone (internal or outsourced) is watching alerts outside business hours.

Incident Response

A written incident response plan exists and names who does what in the first hour.
